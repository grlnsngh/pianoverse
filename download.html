How to install it

The words on your phone may differ a little from maker to maker. The steps are the same.

  1. Tap Download

    Chrome may warn that this type of file can harm your device. Every app from outside the Play Store gets this warning. Tap Download anyway.

  2. Open the file

    When it has finished, tap Open in the message at the bottom of the screen. Or open the Files app, then Downloads, and tap pianoverse.apk.

  3. Allow it, once

    The first time, your phone says it isn’t allowed to install unknown apps from Chrome. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, and go back.

  4. Tap Install

    Google Play Protect may scan the app, or say it doesn’t know it yet, because it isn’t on the Play Store. If you downloaded it from this page, tap Install anyway (you may have to tap More details first). Don’t turn Play Protect off.

  5. Open Pianoverse

    Create your account, or sign in if you already have one. Your pianos are kept in your account, so they are there on any phone you sign in on.

Afterwards you can switch Install unknown apps off again for Chrome. To find it, search your phone’s Settings for “unknown apps”.

After installing

How do updates arrive?

Small updates come by themselves when you open the app; you only need to close it and open it again. When a new version has to be installed, download it from this page and install it over the old one. Nothing is lost: your pianos are in your account.

It says “App not installed”

Most often the phone is short of space: the app needs about 300 MB free while it installs. If an older copy of Pianoverse came from somewhere else, uninstall it first, then install this one and sign in again.

It says “There was a problem parsing the package”

The download didn’t finish. Delete pianoverse.apk from Downloads and download it again, on Wi-Fi if you can. Phones older than Android 7.0 can’t install it.

How do I remove it?

Open Settings, then Apps, then Pianoverse, and tap Uninstall. Your account and pianos stay until you delete your account.

Is it coming to the Play Store?

Yes, a Play Store listing is being prepared. When it is ready, this page will send you there, and updates will come through the Play Store like any other app.
