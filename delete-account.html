Delete your account

You can delete your Pianoverse account, and everything in it, at any time.

In the app

  1. 1
    Open Account

    Open Pianoverse, signed in to the account, and choose the Account tab.

  2. 2
    Choose Delete account

    It is under Privacy, near the bottom.

  3. 3
    Confirm

    Choose Delete account again. Everything is deleted straight away, and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email pianoverse.app.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Pianoverse account”. Send it from the email address of your account, so that nobody can delete someone else’s.

Your account and everything in it will be deleted within 30 days, and you will get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address, password, profile picture and UPI ID.
  • All your pianos and their photos.
  • Your rentals and sales, with your customers’ names and phone numbers.
  • Your rent payments, rental history, tuning and service records and expenses.
  • The count of which paper forms you used.

Nothing is kept, and it can’t be recovered. Copies in the storage service’s own backups, if there are any, expire on their own. Crash reports the app sent, which carry only your account’s ID, are deleted by Sentry after at most 90 days.

On your phone

Deleting in the app also clears what the app kept on that phone. If you deleted by email, uninstalling the app clears it. Files you downloaded or shared from the app stay wherever you saved them.

Read more in the privacy policy.
