Forgot your password?

  1. 1
    Ask for a link

    In the app, choose Sign in, then Forgot password, and enter your email.

  2. 2
    Open it from your email

    It comes from Pianoverse. Check your spam folder if you can’t find it.

  3. 3
    Choose a new password

    Use at least 8 characters, then sign in to the app with it.

For your safety, each link works once and stops working after a while. You can always ask for a new one.
