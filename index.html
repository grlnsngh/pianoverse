For businesses that rent and sell pianos

Every piano, every rental, in one place.

Pianoverse keeps your pianos, the people who rent them and the rent they pay in one list on your phone. Each morning it tells you who owes what, which rentals end this week and which pianos need tuning.

Download for Android

Free. Version 1.1.20, 147 MB, for Android 7.0 or later.

  • Made with a piano rental business. Built around how a family firm in India rents pianos to homes, schools and hotels.
  • Free, no ads. No subscription and nothing to buy inside the app.
  • Yours alone. Your pianos and customers are seen only when you sign in to your account.

What it does

One job at a time, the way the day goes.

Rent

Know who owes rent, and since when.

Today lists every rent that has fallen due, oldest first, with the renter’s name and the amount: Meera Kapoor, ₹7,000, two months. Record a payment in two taps, or send a polite reminder on WhatsApp with your UPI ID already in it.

Each piano

Everything about one piano on one page.

Its photos, who has it and until when, the deposit, what it cost and what it has earned, and when it was last tuned. Extend the rental, mark it returned or sell it from the same page.

Money

What came in this month, and what went out.

Rent received by month and by piano, the shop’s own costs, and what each piano has made after its price. Download it all as a spreadsheet for your accountant.

Deposits

The deposits you hold, and what to give back.

Note the deposit when a piano goes out. When it comes back, Pianoverse works out the refund, less any rent unpaid or damage, and keeps a record.

Calendar

Which pianos are free on a given day.

Pick a day to see which pianos are out, which are free, and which come back soon, before you promise one to a customer.

Tuning

Tunings and repairs, with a reminder.

Each piano has its own tuning interval. Today shows what is due this week, and your phone reminds you at 9:00 in the morning.

Paper

Forms and agreements on your letterhead.

Rent cards, receipts, a handover checklist and a two-page rental agreement with your own terms, printed on your letterhead. For days when pen and paper is easier.

Labels

A label inside every piano.

Print QR stickers on ordinary label sheets, stick one inside each lid, and scan it with the app to open that piano straight away.

Good to know

Big text is fine
Every screen is built and checked with the phone’s Font size at 150% and more.
Updates arrive by themselves
Small updates come when you open the app. You are only asked to install a new version for bigger changes.
Your data
Stored with Appwrite and shown only to your account. Read the privacy policy, or delete your account at any time.
Android only, for now
It runs on Android 7.0 or later. An iPhone version may come later.

Questions

Is it on the Google Play Store?

Not yet. Until it is, you install it from the download page. When it reaches the Play Store, this page will link there.

Can my staff use it too?

Today each account sees only its own pianos, so a business uses one account. Accounts for staff that share the same pianos are planned.

I forgot my password
  1. In the app, choose Sign in, then Forgot password, and enter your email.
  2. Open the email from Pianoverse. Check your spam folder if you can’t find it.
  3. Choose a new password of at least 8 characters, then sign in with it.

Each link works once and stops working after a while. You can always ask for a new one.

How do I delete my account and my data?

From the app’s Account tab, or by email. The delete-account page has both ways.
