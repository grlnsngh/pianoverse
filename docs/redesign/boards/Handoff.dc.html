Rules the screens assume
Primary action by piano state
Rented, active or overdue: Record payment
On sale: Mark as sold
Events or Warehouse: Edit piano
Sold: Undo sale
Rental status text
End date passed: Overdue · 18 days, or months from 60 days
7 days or fewer left: Ends in 3 days
Otherwise: 12 days left, 2 months left
Overdue and ending soon also show as a badge on the photo.
Rent progress bar
Elapsed time divided by rental length, capped at full. On the detail page any time past the end is a red segment.
Needs attention order
Overdue first, longest overdue on top, then rentals ending within 7 days, soonest first.
Today totals
Received: payments dated in the current month. Sold: sale date in the current month. On rent: rentals that have not ended.
Money and dates
₹ with Indian grouping (en-IN), tabular figures. Dates as 21 Dec 2025.

Behaviour
Loading: skeletons after 200 ms on Today, Pianos and Detail. Keys loader for full-screen waits, spinner inside buttons. See States and feedback.
Motion: timings and easing are on the Motion board. Reduced motion means fades only.
Errors: field errors on blur or submit, a toast with Retry for failed saves, a full screen with Try again when a list cannot load.
Offline: a slim strip above the list, “Offline. Showing pianos saved on 28 Sep.”
Permissions: explain reminders before the system prompt. If camera is denied, offer Settings or the gallery.
Dialogs: the destructive choice is red and on top, the safe choice is last. Six are drawn.
Gestures: tap a photo card to open, long-press to select, swipe a list row for Edit and Delete, pull down to refresh.
