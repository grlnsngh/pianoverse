Pianoverse
Design language, final
Photo first, like the best listing and rental apps. Progress and status are shown, not described. One brand colour, one clear action per screen.
Principles
Photo first
Pianos are recognised by sight. Photos lead in the grid, the shelves and the detail page (Airbnb, Turo).
Progress you can see
A rental is a bar that fills toward its end date, red once it runs over (Netflix, Apple TV).
One action, always in reach
A sticky bar carries the next step for that piano: record payment, mark as sold, edit or undo.
Colour is a signal
Red is late. Orange text is ending soon. Brand orange is for the main action only.
Numbers lead
Amounts and dates are the largest text on their screen, in tabular figures, Indian digit grouping.
One screen per step
Add steps fit without scrolling. Sheets ask for four fields or fewer.
